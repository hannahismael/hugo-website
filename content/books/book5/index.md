#### Description

AI policy debates often stall on words that mean different things to different people. *EncyclopAIdia* is a glossary developed with researchers at Carnegie Mellon University's CyLab that traces the etymology, definitional distinctions, and policy implications of contested terms and concepts in AI governance.

I first-authored two entries: a guide on **fair use**, analyzing how divergent legal rulings could shape AI training, licensing regimes, creative labor, unions, and the future of work; and a guide on **AI benefit sharing**, taxonomizing benefit-sharing mechanisms and actors across sovereignty, conditional access, and profit redistribution frameworks.

---

#### Some Questions I Explored:

- Where do legal and technical usages of the same governance term diverge, and what does that divergence cost in practice?

- How would competing fair use outcomes redistribute value between model developers and creative labor?

- What mechanisms actually distribute the benefits of AI systems, and to whom do they accrue?
