#### Description

In light of AI fundamentally changing how artists' work is valued and created, we host [Mozilla's Imaginative Intelligences](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/creative-futures-imaginative-intelligences/), a series of participatory assemblies and technology residencies bringing artists, technologists, researchers, policymakers, and media executives together to discuss consent, attribution, and compensation. The goal is to bring creatives, from across media sectors, to the forefront of these discussions so that future policy meaningfully addresses their concerns.

The programme has run across San Francisco, Mumbai, New York City, Berlin, and Guangzhou, producing public reports and governance recommendations on AI-generated content, attribution, transparency, and platform accountability. Findings were presented at the Participatory AI Research Symposium at the 2026 India AI Impact Summit.

This page will be updated as the work develops.
